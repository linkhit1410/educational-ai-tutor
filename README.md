# Educational AI Tutor Prototype

This project explores how local language models can be used to support programming education. The goal is to design an AI tutor that helps students learn through hints, debugging guidance, and reflective questions rather than simply providing complete answers.

## Week 1 Focus

Week 1 focuses on the foundations of educational AI tutoring. I explored why one-on-one tutoring is effective, the role of educational scaffolding, and the benefits and risks of AI in programming education.

## Technical Exploration

I installed and tested Ollama with the `llama3.2` model. I asked beginner-level Java questions about inheritance, NullPointerExceptions, and homework completion to evaluate whether the model behaved like a tutor or a general chatbot.

## Key Findings

The model was useful for explaining concepts and giving debugging suggestions, but it also made technical mistakes and sometimes provided too much help. This suggests that an educational AI tutor needs stronger design guidelines, such as asking students what they have tried, giving hints before solutions, and avoiding complete assignment answers.
