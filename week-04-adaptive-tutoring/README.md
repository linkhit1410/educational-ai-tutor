# Week 4: Designing Adaptive AI Tutors

## Purpose

Week 4 focuses on adaptive pedagogical guardrails. The goal is to design a tutor that does not give every student the same kind of help.

Instead of only asking, "What is the correct answer?", the tutor should ask:

"What type of support will help this student learn the most?"

This week builds on Week 3. In Week 3, the tutor began detecting tutoring scenarios such as concept learning, debugging, assignment clarification, and reflection. In Week 4, the tutor begins deciding how much help to provide based on student intent, demonstrated effort, and academic-integrity risk.

## Main Research Idea

An effective programming tutor should adapt its response based on:

- What the student is trying to accomplish
- How much effort the student has already shown
- Whether the request is safe or risky
- What type of support will promote learning

## Week 4 Goals

- Design multiple levels of support
- Classify student intent
- Analyze student effort
- Create an adaptive decision framework
- Begin integrating adaptive decision-making into the tutor
- Evaluate whether adaptive behavior improves tutoring quality

## Key Shift

Week 3 question:

What kind of tutoring situation is this?

Week 4 question:

How much help should the tutor provide for this student in this situation?
